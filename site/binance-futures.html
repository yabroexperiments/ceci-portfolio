01My Role

Product Designer

The Trading Platform was redesigned in 2021 with my design layout and style, which was selected among the designer proposals in an internal competition.

I am the owner of Binance Futures Battle, a gamification product introduced in 2021 with 50K+ users at launch — developed from scratch to launch within 3 weeks with PM, Dev and Marketing teams.

I am also the owner of the Futures Wallet and Orders pages, Futures Landing pages, the Cooling-Off Period function, and Binance Convert — a critical product allowing users to convert between a wide range of tokens.

02Trading Page Redesign

Rebuilding the Futures trading page around clearer structure, customization, and new-user confidence.

Before — the 2021 platform

  1. Space usage and blocks were not clear enough.
  2. The market selection area was unclear.
  3. No unified title for each block.
  4. Red and green numbers were poorly readable.
  5. No custom functions.
  6. The navigation bar needed to be unified across the entire site.
  7. Hard to find where to change Leverage & Margin Mode.
  8. Not friendly to new users or beginners.
2021 Trading page — before redesign

After — the 2022 redesign

  1. Reduced dividing lines; redefined block positions and proportions.
  2. Rearranged the visual flow for users.
  3. Unified block design such as titles and icons.
  4. Redefined colors to avoid eye fatigue during long sessions.
  5. Enabled users to customize block content and size.
  6. Added a navigation bar, unifying the experience with other pages.
  7. Unified the control area into the right panel.
  8. Improved the experience for new users and beginners.
2022 Trading page — after redesign
