01My Role

I worked closely with PMs and engineers to translate complex DeFi requirements into clear, scalable product experiences. My primary responsibility was UI/UX design — information hierarchy, interaction patterns, user guidance, responsive layouts, and final visual execution across web and mobile.

Beyond designing the product itself, I conducted extensive competitor research before starting the project. In the DEX space, users are often attracted by high yields, but many platforms still lack the trust and user experience that centralized exchanges provide.

My focus was on simplifying complex information and making the experience more approachable for both new and experienced users. I worked closely with the PM through multiple design iterations, and gathered community feedback through simple social media surveys to continuously improve the experience.

02Context

Depositing is the first meaningful step

After connecting a wallet, the next major challenge was helping users confidently make their first deposit. On Drift, deposited assets can also be used as collateral for perpetual trading, making the Earn experience closely connected to user activation, liquidity, and the broader trading ecosystem.

Connect Wallet

Redesigned the wallet connection experience to reduce complexity, prioritize common wallets, and lower the barrier for new Web3 users.

Deposit Assets

Improved APY visibility, added multiple deposit entry points, and simplified technical guidance to reduce friction for new users.

Earn Yield

Helped users manage assets, build trust, and discover earning opportunities like Isolated Pools and dSOL liquid staking.

Trading Collateral

Since any deposited asset can be used as collateral, Borrow/Lend focused on making account health and borrowing APY easy to understand.

06Isolated Pools

Isolated pools introduced more flexibility but also created confusion for users unfamiliar with DeFi mechanics.

My Design Focus

1

Clarified the differences between isolated products

Clearly distinguished Borrow & Lend Isolated Pools, Amplify, dSOL Liquid Staking, DRIFT Staking and Vaults, helping users understand that assets in these products could not be used as trading collateral.

2

Reduced the learning curve for DeFi concepts

Introduced contextual onboarding, tooltips, and inline explanations to make complex concepts such as isolated pools, collateral, and subaccounts easier to understand.

3

Simplified specialized Earn products

Improved guidance for managing liabilities, making it easier for users to understand how to repay borrowed assets and monitor loan status.

4

Provided a clearer financial overview

Improved the visibility of earnings, deposited assets, liabilities, and available collateral, so users could quickly understand their overall financial position.

07Deposit and Onboarding

Connecting a wallet did not always lead to a completed deposit. For new users, cross-chain funding flows were often too complex. We wanted to reduce drop-off right after onboarding and help users complete their first deposit with confidence.

My Design Focus

  1. Simplified the onboarding flow by removing unnecessary steps and reducing cognitive load.
  2. Added clearer and more visible entry points for deposits.
  3. Guided users through cross-chain deposit steps with less technical complexity.
  4. Educated users that deposited assets could earn yield and be used as collateral for perpetual trading.
